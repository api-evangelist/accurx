# Accurx

> Accurx is a UK clinical communication and workflow company (London, founded 2016) whose software is used across NHS primary, secondary, and community/mental-health care to message patients, run patient triage, batch-message cohorts, book and remind appointments, and draft clinical notes with AI (Scribe). Accurx integrates around the NHS as an assured IM1 live supplier — consuming NHS Digital interoperability interfaces and connecting bi-directionally to the GP clinical-system duopoly (EMIS Health, TPP SystmOne) and Vision, plus NHS national services (PDS, NHS Single Sign-On, NHS Login, NHS App). It is a FHIR/IM1 consumer and integrator, not a publisher of a public developer API: as of this profile there is no public developer portal, no self-serve REST/FHIR API, and no downloadable OpenAPI or FHIR CapabilityStatement.

## Note for agents
Accurx does not expose a public API. Integrations are partner/trust-gated and arranged under agreement through the partnerships and integration teams. Any NHS FHIR/IM1 CapabilityStatement lives on the NHS England platform (api.service.nhs.uk / digital.nhs.uk), not on an Accurx host.
